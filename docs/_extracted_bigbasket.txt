This Is Not a Bill-Scanning Platform  |  It Is a Contract-to-Payment Control System
Capture is the smallest part of what happens. The value sits in what the platform does after the bill arrives — validate it against the contract, enforce segregation of duties, and post only what is provably correct.
01
Agreement as Source of Truth
Every rental agreement and freight contract is digitised into structured, machine-readable terms — rent, escalation clause, escalation date, lock-in, notice period, tax treatment, rate cards. The agreement becomes an active rule, not a stored PDF.
02
Multi-Channel Capture
Landlords and transporters submit however they already work — email, PDF, scanned image, or the Supplier Portal. AI extracts structured data from any format. No behaviour change is forced on 3,000+ suppliers.
03
Automated Validation
Each bill is auto-matched against its own agreement. Rent amount, escalation applied, period, GST, TDS. Correct bills pass. Deviations are flagged with the reason and the clause they breach — before a human ever sees them.
04
Maker–Checker Governance
Verified bills route to the Maker inbox with the exception already explained. Maker submits; Checker approves. Segregation of duties is enforced by the system, not by convention. Every action is time-stamped and attributable.
05
Controlled ERP Posting
Only fully approved bills reach Oracle Fusion — via file batch in Phase 1, via API when your ERP team is ready. The platform is the control gate; the ERP receives only clean, approved, reconciled data.
06
Audit &amp; Intelligence
Complete audit trail per bill: who captured, who verified, who approved, against which clause. Plus escalation calendars, accrual visibility, exception analytics and landlord/transporter performance.
Scanning captures an image. This platform enforces a contract.

Use Case Flow  |  Rental Bill — Agreement to Oracle Fusion
1,500 locations, every month. Below is the complete lifecycle of a single rental bill through the platform.
1
Agreement Onboarding
Agreement key data uploaded — single or bulk. Rent, escalation %, escalation date, lock-in, GST/TDS captured as structured rules.
›
2
Invoice Capture
Landlord emails the bill, uploads on the Supplier Portal, or platform self-bills from the agreement. AI extracts from any format.
›
3
Auto-Validation
Bill auto-matched to its agreement. Rent, escalation, period, GST/TDS, duplicates checked. Deviations flagged with the clause breached.
›
4
Maker Verification
Bill lands in Maker inbox with exception reason pre-attached. Reviewed against the agreement side-by-side, then submitted.
›
5
Checker Approval
Checker sees bill, clause, Maker action and full context. Approve, reject or return. Segregation of duties system-enforced.
›
6
ERP Posting
Posted to Oracle Fusion. Phase 1: structured file download. Phase 2: direct API. Audit trail sealed.
Escalation clauses are the hidden risk. The platform tracks every escalation date across 1,500 agreements and applies the increase automatically — so you never overpay on a stale rate, and never underpay into a dispute.
